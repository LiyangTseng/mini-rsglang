<objective>
Build the configurable load generator behind BENCH-02 (D-01: one driver with closed and open modes and shared hdrhistogram recording). It has three parts:
- Scenario 1's seeded closed-loop agents with think time and client-side cancellation (D-02, BENCH-03).
- An open-loop Poisson driver and a fixed-concurrency closed driver for Scenario 2.
- The confidence-interval statistics that D-05's run-to-run comparison uses (BENCH-07).

Purpose: the orchestrator (07-06) and the S2 runner (07-07) call these drivers once per trial. The statistics module turns N per-trial values into mean ± 95% CI and Rust-vs-Python deltas.

Output: rng.rs, loadgen.rs, stats.rs, the metrics.rs additions, and tests loadgen_cancel.rs and metrics.rs.

Design notes (Claude's discretion):
- The PRNG is hand-written SplitMix64, not the `rand` crate. Its output is a fixed published algorithm, so a seed recorded in a manifest (D-07) regenerates the same workload regardless of crate versions. `rand` also is not in the RESEARCH package audit.
- Prompts are words from Phase 2's 64-word list. The harness has no tokenizer, and both frontends receive identical prompt text for a given seed, which is what fairness needs.
- Raw histograms are stored as hex-encoded HdrHistogram V2-deflate bytes. That is the standard binary form hdrhistogram's own Deserializer reads, and it needs no base64 crate.
</objective>
