## Flagged Assumptions (edge probe, unresolved)

The spec-less edge probe returned `unclassified` for BASE-02 and BASE-03. Per protocol these stay unresolved and are surfaced here as explicit assumptions rather than dropped:

- **BASE-02 (unclassified):** two concurrent rust-mode launches on one host do not interfere at the IPC layer because each run's sockets carry its own `.rsg=<launcher pid>` suffix and the launcher only unlinks its own five paths. Two launches that share an upstream `--port` still collide on upstream's TCP distributed address; that is upstream behavior and out of scope.
- **BASE-03 (unclassified):** the handshake is sent exactly once per run, only after TP rank 0 reports ready; ranks above 0 never report, and a backend that never becomes ready is handled by `--ready-timeout` (default 900 s), never by a partial or repeated handshake.
