1. Spawn the mock with `--decode-delay-ms 20 --misbehave-uids 5 --behavior late-abort-token`, then wait_ready, `frontend().split()`, spawn_writer and spawn_dispatcher.
2. In a 4-worker multi-thread runtime `block_on`:
   - register uids 5 and 6. Move uid 6's UidStream into a spawned consumer task that pushes every event into a Vec until recv returns None, then returns the Vec. It drains continuously, so uid 6 never lags (its channel holds only 16).
   - From the main task, submit uid 5 (ids [51,52,53], max_tokens 1000) and then uid 6 (ids [61,62,63], max_tokens 1000).
   - Wait for uid 5's first Token. Then call `dispatch.deregister(5)` followed by `writer.abort(&t5)`.
   - Poll `dispatch.stats()` every 10 ms until unknown_uid >= 3 (2 s deadline). Wait 200 ms more and assert unknown_uid <= 4: 3 late tokens plus at most one already in flight. Check this bound now, before uid 6 is aborted, because uid 6's own in-flight tokens after its deregister would also count as unknown.
   - Drain uid 5's stream until None (1 s timeout); no event in it has finished=true.
   - Call `writer.abort(&t6)` and then `dispatch.deregister(6)`. Await the consumer task, which ends when uid 6's stream returns None. Its events contain no Dropped and no finished token, number at least 5 Tokens (uid 6 kept streaming throughout uid 5's abort), and equal the prefix of echo_tokens([61,62,63], n) for the count n received.
   - Call `writer.exit()`.
3. Outside the runtime:
   - wait_exit is 0;
   - observed() == [Submit{5,3}, Submit{6,3}, Abort{5}, Abort{6}, Exit];
   - the mock's stderr contains "late-abort-token";
   - the final stats show malformed_frames == 0.
