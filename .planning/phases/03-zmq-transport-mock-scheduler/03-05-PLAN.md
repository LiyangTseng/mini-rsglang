<objective>
Complete the per-uid dispatcher's contract (WIRE-03, success criterion 4):
- a slow consumer on one request never stalls the others;
- every dropped token is counted and surfaced (D-05, D-06);
- replies for unknown or deregistered uids, and malformed frames, are dropped and counted without crashing (D-04);
- `deregister` gives Phase 5 explicit route cleanup.

Purpose: CONTEXT records why the drop accounting matters. A Phase 6 parity debugging session must never confuse "we dropped a backed-up token on purpose" with "the backend did something wrong." The stats counters are also what lets 03-06, and later LIFE-02, prove that late tokens after an abort were dropped and counted, not just ignored.

Output: the extended `src/dispatch.rs` (UidStream::dropped, DispatchStats, DispatchStatsSnapshot, DispatchHandle::{deregister, stats}, unit tests) and `tests/dispatch_backpressure.rs`.

The capacity stays the fixed constant UID_CHANNEL_CAPACITY = 16 (D-07): no knob. Unknown-uid drops log at debug, not warn: after a mass cancellation they can number in the thousands and would flood the log. The counter is their signal.
</objective>

So all 200 of uid 1's replies have been routed by the time uid 2's finished token is read, while uid 1's channel keeps only the newest 16. If the executor finds these exact counts flaky, it must not loosen them silently: it should fix the cause or report it.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| scheduler (or mock) → rx-zmq dispatcher | Peer-controlled detok frames, including uids the frontend never issued and arbitrary bytes |
| rx-zmq dispatcher → per-request consumers | Consumers drain at their own pace and may stop entirely |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-15 | Repudiation | silent gaps in a uid's token stream (a backpressure drop misattributed to the backend) | medium | mitigate | In-band Dropped(n), the per-uid dropped() counter and a structured warn (D-06). Proven by lagged_stream_reports_dropped_event_counter_and_warning and the end-to-end tracer |
| T-03-16 | Denial of Service | malformed or hostile detok frames | medium | mitigate | Decode is matched on Result. Bad frames are counted in malformed_frames and skipped, and the thread keeps routing (malformed_frame_is_skipped_and_dispatcher_keeps_routing) |
| T-03-17 | Denial of Service | route-table growth from aborted or abandoned requests | low | mitigate | Routes are removed on finished, on a failed send to a dropped stream (closed_route), and by explicit deregister. Unknown uids never create state |
| T-03-18 | Spoofing | replies for uids the frontend never registered | low | mitigate | Dropped and counted in unknown_uid without touching any other route (unknown_uid_reply_is_dropped_and_counted) |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No new packages. tokio, rustc-hash and tracing-subscriber are already pinned, and the 03-01 audit covers the workspace |
